Overview

Helping people confidently restart their careers

Career Restart & Learning is a mobile app designed for professionals who've taken a break — through layoffs, parenthood, health, or a career pivot — and want a structured, encouraging path back into work. The app turns an overwhelming 'where do I even start' feeling into a clear, personalized learning journey: pick a goal, follow a recommended programme, complete bite-sized lessons and assignments, and track visible progress toward being job-ready again.

Problem

Restarting a career feels directionless and discouraging

People returning to work after a gap often don't lack ability — they lack a clear starting point. Existing learning platforms are built for people who already know what they want to learn. For someone restarting, the real problem isn't content, it's direction, confidence, and momentum. Early research and self-review of the assignment brief surfaced three consistent gaps in comparable products.

What we learned

The strongest pattern across comparable products and the assignment goals was that motivation fades fast without visible momentum. Users restarting their careers need reassurance at every step — not just content, but confirmation they're making real progress.

'Restart' doesn't mean 'start from zero' — I just needed someone to tell me what to do first, and to show me I was actually getting somewhere.

— Design assumption, based on target user research

Organizing recurring patterns and behaviors

I used these insights to identify where users experienced the most friction across the learning experience, and where small design choices could rebuild confidence quickly.

Goals need translation

A vague ambition should turn into a concrete, named programme in one step

Progress must be visible

Learners need to see how far they've come, not just what's left

Small wins matter

Bite-sized tasks and quick completions rebuild confidence faster than long courses

Guidance over choice

Fewer, clearer decisions beat open-ended course catalogs

Design

Refining the app experience

The final designs focus on a warm, encouraging visual tone — clear typography, generous spacing, and a progress-forward layout — so the product feels supportive rather than overwhelming for someone rebuilding their confidence.

Final hi-fi — onboarding & dashboard

Every core screen — onboarding, programme recommendation, learning dashboard, assignment view, and progress tracker — was designed to keep the next action obvious and progress visible at a glance.

Final hi-fi — assignment & progress screens
